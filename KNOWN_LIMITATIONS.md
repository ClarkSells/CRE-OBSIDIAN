# Known Limitations

The canonical limitations and scope guide is [docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).

The important framing is that most listed items are intentional V2 product boundaries, not demo blockers. V2 succeeds as a polished, local-first Obsidian cockpit that safely demonstrates broker workflows, provenance, data quality, focused graph and map intelligence, a controlled RealNex CSV bridge, and the review layer for future external research agents.
